# AshantiHub — Customer Script

Promo video voiceover (60–90s) and three short social clips (30–45s) aimed at customers.
The companion script for businesses is [`business-script.md`](business-script.md).

---

## Who we are

**Concept.** AshantiHub is *the Marketplace of Ashanti*: one platform where customers find,
buy from, book and get tickets from the businesses of Kumasi and the Ashanti Region, and every
one of those businesses has been verified before it can sell.

**Vision.** An Asanteman where every
business, from the Kejetia stall to the Suame workshop, can be found, trusted and financed by
customers at home and across the world.

**Mission** *(as published on the About page)*. To digitise the informal economy of Asanteman —
expanding customer bases, unlocking access to credit, and creating jobs across the region, with
women entrepreneurs at the centre of everything we do.

## Production notes — keep it honest

These scripts only claim what the platform does today. When editing or adapting them:

- **No direct contact with businesses.** Never show or say "WhatsApp the seller" or "chat with the
  business". Every enquiry is *AshantiHub Support about* a business.
- **Don't name payment providers** (Mobile Money, Hubtel, card). In-app payment is not yet live
  with a real provider.
- **No app-store buttons or "download the app".** There is no mobile app yet; every CTA goes to
  theashantihub.com.
- **No currency-switching claims.** Prices are shown in cedis; the currency selector was removed.
- **No statistics** (business counts, customer counts, artisan numbers) unless they come from a
  source you can cite.
- **Filmed for real.** Every scene is live action in Kumasi and Bonwire, captured as set out in
  [`shoot-package.md`](shoot-package.md). No screen recordings, no scrolling and no illustrated
  or composited UI: the app appears only as a short phone insert filmed in camera, where the
  words on screen carry the claim. The only graphics are text supers and the end card. One host
  fronts the social clips and voices every promo and clip.
- **Akosua Ntoma is a fictional demo business** (a Bonwire kente weaver) set up on staging for
  these videos and portrayed on camera by a Bonwire weaver; the demo service business is a demo
  store too. Every phone insert showing a demo store carries a *Demo store* super, and every end
  card carries one line of fine print per demo store in that video: *"Akosua Ntoma is a
  demonstration store, portrayed by a Bonwire weaver."*, and the same form, with the name it has
  on staging, for each other demo store. Swap in real AshantiHub businesses only with their
  permission.
- **Formats:** 9:16 vertical for iOS and Android (Reels, TikTok, Shorts, WhatsApp Status) and
  16:9 for the web, both H.264 MP4 with captions burned in.
- The example products and services (kente, barbers, braiders, caterers, fabric) are
  illustrative. Before filming, swap in categories that are actually listed on the site.
