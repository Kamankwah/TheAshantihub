### Content rules (non-negotiable)

- **Real Kumasi footage only.** Real places, real people, real work. No stock "Africa" footage and no AI-generated people or places (DESIGN.md: *real Kumasi photography only*).
- **Every identifiable person and business signs a release** before footage is used (checklist below).
- **Akosua Ntoma is a fictional demo store.** Any screen showing it carries the "Demo store" label. A real weaver filmed for the business promo either portrays the demo store under a talent release, or — better — is a real Bonwire business that joins AshantiHub and features her own storefront.
- **No direct contact with businesses.** Never show anyone WhatsApp-ing, calling or chatting with a seller. Enquiries go to AshantiHub Support.
- **No payment brands** (Mobile Money, Hubtel, card logos), **no app-store buttons or "download the app"**, no third-party platform branding (Facebook, Instagram, Google, bank logos) on any screen or signboard in shot.
- **No invented numbers.** Prices only as in the scripts — "plans from GHS 10/month", "first billing cycle free". Lending partners are "coming soon". Counts on screen (orders, tickets) come from real staging data, never typed in.

## Audio

- **No music on set.** Music and voiceover are added in the edit; the VO is recorded separately.
- Capture clean **natural sound** with every live shot: loom shuttle clack, market hubbub, spanner on metal, pot stirring and sizzle, motorbike arriving.
- Record **30s of room tone** at every location (everyone silent, same mic position).
- No talking behind the camera during takes. Note any noisy takes in the log.

## Delivery spec

| | |
|---|---|
| **Resolution** | 3840×2160 (4K UHD) preferred, 1920×1080 minimum. Vertical takes 2160×3840 or 1080×1920 |
| **Frame rate** | **30 fps** for everything (the edit timeline is 30 fps — don't mix 25 and 30) |
| **Shutter** | 1/60 outdoors; **1/50 or 1/100 under indoor mains lighting** to avoid 50 Hz flicker |
| **Codec** | H.264/H.265 at ≥ 50 Mbps, or ProRes. Flat or log profile optional — if log, name the LUT |
| **Audio** | 48 kHz, on-camera or separate recorder; room tone as separate `.wav` files |
| **File naming** | `promo-customer_shot05_take2.mp4`, `promo-business_shot01_take1_v.mp4` (`_v` = vertical take), `promo-business_roomtone_bonwire.wav` |
| **Hand-off** | One folder per promo, plus a `releases/` folder and the shot log (shot # · take · notes · best take) |
