# AshantiHub — Business Script

Promo video voiceover (60–90s) and three short social clips (30–45s) aimed at business owners.
The companion script for customers, with the full concept, vision and mission, is
[`customer-script.md`](customer-script.md).

---

## Who we are

**Concept.** AshantiHub is *the Marketplace of Ashanti*: one verified platform that puts the
businesses of Kumasi and the Ashanti Region in front of customers at home and across the world,
then helps them sell, deliver, and build the track record that unlocks credit.

**Mission.** To digitise the informal economy of Asanteman — expanding customer bases, unlocking
access to credit, and creating jobs across the region, with women entrepreneurs at the centre of
everything we do.

---

## Why sign on — at a glance

1. **Nothing to pay to join.** Your first billing cycle is free on every plan.
2. **Customers beyond your street.** Buyers across Ghana and the diaspora can find you.
3. **Sell three ways from one page.** Products with cart and checkout, service bookings, and event
   tickets with gate check-in.
4. **Delivery handled.** AshantiHub coordinates delivery of your orders.
5. **We take the enquiries.** AshantiHub Support answers customer questions about your business,
   so your private number stays private and time-wasters never reach you.
6. **Trust that sells.** Every business is verified, and reviews are checked before they go live,
   so the good name you earn on AshantiHub is believed.
7. **Get seen.** Every plan makes you eligible for the homepage Hero spotlight, and boosts push
   your listings further.
8. **A record the bank can't ignore.** Your orders, reviews and activity build an AshantiHub
   Business Credit Score from day one. Lending partners are coming as a future add-on, and your
   score is the track record you'll apply with.
9. **One dashboard.** Listings, orders, bookings, deliveries, events, reviews, credit and your
   plan, all in one place.

## Production notes — keep it honest

These scripts only claim what the platform does today. When editing or adapting them:

- **"Plans from GHS 10/month" means product plans.** The Service plan is GHS 150/month. Don't
  imply ten cedis to a service-business audience, and confirm current prices before publishing:
  plans can be edited in the admin.
- **The free offer is the first billing cycle**, not "free forever" and not "3 months free".
- **Lending partners are a future add-on.** The credit score is live; loans are not. Always say
  partners are *coming*, and never promise a loan, an amount or an approval.
- **The Hero spotlight is eligibility, not a guarantee.** Hero placements are approved by
  AshantiHub staff.
- **No direct contact with businesses.** Never show a customer WhatsApp-ing or chatting with a
  business directly. Enquiries go through AshantiHub Support.
- **Don't name payment providers** (Mobile Money, Hubtel, card). In-app payment is not yet live
  with a real provider.
- **No app-store buttons or "download the app".** There is no mobile app yet; every CTA goes to
  theashantihub.com.
- **No statistics** (customer numbers, sales uplift, business counts) unless they come from a
  source you can cite.
- **Akosua Ntoma is a fictional demo business** (a Bonwire kente weaver) set up on staging for
  these videos. Any screen showing it carries a small "Demo store" label. Feature real AshantiHub
  businesses only with their permission.
- **Formats:** 9:16 vertical for iOS and Android (Reels, TikTok, Shorts, WhatsApp Status) and
  16:9 for the web, both H.264 MP4 with captions burned in.
