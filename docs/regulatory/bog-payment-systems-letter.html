REQUEST FOR REGULATORY GUIDANCE AND NO-OBJECTION: USE OF HUBTEL AS PAYMENT AGGREGATOR AND FUNDS-HOLDING PARTNER FOR THE ASHANTIHUB CUSTOMER WALLET

1. Introduction

[AshantiHub legal name, registration number, date of incorporation] ("AshantiHub") operates an online marketplace connecting customers in the Ashanti Region with local businesses, service providers, event organisers and stays, with delivery coordinated through the platform. We write to seek the Bank of Ghana's guidance on, and no-objection to, the payment structure described below before we make any wallet or escrow feature available to the public. We understand that access to a licensed provider's collection and disbursement services for this purpose depends on a letter from the Bank.

2. What we are asking for

We intend to offer customers and vendors a wallet within AshantiHub, with all underlying money held and moved by a Bank of Ghana-licensed payment service provider [provider's legal name and licence category, to be confirmed in writing by the provider] (the "Provider") acting as our payment aggregator. We respectfully request the Bank's confirmation of:

  1. whether this structure is permissible under the Provider's existing authorisation, or whether AshantiHub requires its own licence, authorisation or registration under the Payment Systems and Services Act, 2019 (Act 987) and related directives;
  2. any conditions, limits or reporting obligations the Bank would attach, including applicable wallet balance and transaction limits; and
  3. a meeting with the Department, if helpful, so that we can present the design and our controls in detail.

3. What we propose to do

  • AshantiHub does not hold customer or vendor money. All funds collected for an order are held by the Provider in its designated account. No customer funds are placed in an AshantiHub-owned bank or mobile-money account.
  • The AshantiHub wallet is a ledger, not a store of funds. It is a double-entry record of who is entitled to what, backed one-for-one by funds held at the Provider. A customer's wallet shows refunds and credits owed to them; a vendor's wallet shows money that is Pending (paid but not yet released) or Available (released and withdrawable).
  • Money moves only through the Provider. Customers pay through the Provider's checkout (mobile money or card). Vendors and riders are paid out by the Provider to their own verified accounts. AshantiHub sends instructions; the Provider executes them.
  • Funds are released only after delivery is verified. A vendor's money is released once the customer has confirmed receipt and an AshantiHub Support officer has completed a verification call with no dispute. Disputes are settled within 72 hours of purchase, and an open dispute blocks release.
  • Our platform fee is deducted at release, as a disclosed percentage or flat amount per order. Fee rules are published and changes need approval from two separate staff roles.

The wallet, ledger and payout features are designed and have been developed on simulated payment rails.
