## 2. What AshantiHub is

AshantiHub is an online marketplace where customers in the Ashanti Region buy products, book
services, attend events and arrange stays from verified local businesses ("**vendors**"). We run the
marketplace, verify vendors, coordinate delivery, take payment, hold it safely until an order is
confirmed, and pay vendors.

3.1 You must be at least 18 and able to enter a binding contract. Give true information and keep it
up to date.

3.2 We confirm your phone number or email with a one-time code when you register. We will ask for a
code again when you sign in (always for vendors; for customers on a new device or after a period

withdrawing money.

3.3 Keep your password, codes and device safe. **Never share a code with anyone, including someone
who says they are from AshantiHub.** We will never ask you for a code by phone or message. You are
responsible for what happens through your account unless you told us promptly that it was
compromised.

3.4 One person, one account. You may not sell or transfer your account.

## 4. No direct contact between customers and businesses

To protect everyone from fraud, **customers and vendors cannot contact each other directly**
through AshantiHub. All communication goes through **AshantiHub Support**, in a conversation about
the business or listing, and we pass on what is needed. Do not ask for, or give out, personal phone
numbers, bank or mobile money details to arrange anything outside AshantiHub. If someone asks you
to pay or be paid outside AshantiHub, report it to Support. Money paid outside AshantiHub is not
protected by these terms.

## 5. How money moves

5.1 All payments for AshantiHub orders go through a payment service provider licensed by the Bank
of Ghana (the "**Provider**") [[Provider's name]]. **AshantiHub does not hold your money in an
AshantiHub bank or mobile money account.** The Provider holds it. We keep a record (the "**ledger**")
of who is entitled to what and instruct the Provider.

5.2 When a customer pays for an order, the money is **held, not paid to the vendor**, until the
order is delivered and we have verified it (see Part B, section 4 and Part C, section 6).

5.3 Prices and amounts are in Ghana cedis (GHS).

## 6. Acceptable use

You must not: break the law; give false information; use someone else's identity, payment account
or documents; use AshantiHub to launder money or move funds that are not from a genuine order;
attempt to bypass the no-direct-contact rule; interfere with the platform's security; or harass
staff, vendors, customers or riders.

## 7. Suspension and holds

We may suspend an account, restrict a feature, or place a **hold** on money in or owed to an
account when we reasonably suspect fraud, a breach of these terms, a legal requirement, or an
error. We will tell you why unless the law or an investigation prevents us. We may report
suspected offences to the authorities, including the Financial Intelligence Centre, as the law
requires.

# Part B: Customer Terms

## 1. Ordering and price

1.1 You place an order by choosing items and paying. The total shows the items, the **delivery
fee** (added on top, once per order) and, for mobile money or top-ups, a **payment processing**
line.

1.2 We do not add a hidden platform fee to what you pay. Our fee is charged to the vendor.

1.3 Stock is reserved for you for a short time while you pay. If payment does not complete, the
reservation is released.

## 2. Paying

2.1 You can pay by mobile money through the Provider's checkout, or from your **AshantiHub wallet**
(Part D). A wallet payment must cover the whole order; you cannot split an order between the wallet
and mobile money.

2.2 For mobile money payments and top-ups you pay the Provider's **collection charge**, shown as
"payment processing", at cost. There is no such charge for paying from your wallet.

2.3 Your payment is confirmed only when the Provider confirms it to us. Returning to the app after
a payment does not by itself confirm it.

## 3. Delivery and pickup

3.1 Items are delivered by an AshantiHub rider or an independent rider. The delivery fee is
charged once per order.

3.2 **Store pickup:** if you choose pickup, collect your order at the **AshantiHub office within 24
hours** of buying. The AshantiHub Pickup Desk confirms collection. If you do not collect within
that window, we refund the order to your wallet (Part D).

## 4. Confirming your order: the verification call

4.1 After delivery or collection, **please confirm receipt** in the app. A member of AshantiHub
Support will also **call you** to check that what you received matches what you bought. This call
is part of how we protect your money and the vendor's.

4.2 If you tell us the items **match**, we release the payment to the vendor.

4.3 If they **do not match**, tell us on the call. We open a **dispute**. We do not release the
money while a dispute is open. We aim to settle the dispute within **72 hours of your purchase**.
If the dispute is decided in your favour, you are refunded (section 6).

4.4 If we **cannot reach you**, we record the attempt and the order stays open on our list. We will
keep trying. Nothing is released automatically while an order is unverified. Please keep your phone
number up to date and answer calls from AshantiHub Support.

## 5. Cancelling

You can ask Support to cancel an order that has not been handed over. If we cancel a paid order, the
money you paid for the items returns to your wallet (section 6).

## 6. Refunds

6.1 **Refunds always go to your AshantiHub wallet**, and from there you can spend them or withdraw
them (Part D).

6.2 The **delivery fee** returns only when the **whole order** is refunded. A coupon applied to the
order returns to AshantiHub on a whole-order refund.

6.3 A partial refund returns the share of the item price affected, and only a proportionate share of
any payment processing charge.

6.4 The **payment processing charge is not refunded**, except where AshantiHub, the rider or the
vendor made a mistake or was at fault.

6.5 We decide refunds under our dispute process and may ask for evidence such as photos. We will
explain our decision.

# Part C: Vendor Terms

## 1. Becoming and staying a vendor

1.1 To sell you must be **verified**: identity, business registration details and a field visit by
AshantiHub staff to confirm your address. We may re-verify at any time. Your payout details must
match your verified name or registered business name.

1.2 You must give accurate descriptions, prices and stock, honour your listings, keep your prices
and stock current, and not list anything unlawful, counterfeit or unsafe.

1.3 Keep your account secure (Part A, section 3). We require a one-time code every time a vendor
signs in.

## 2. Orders

When a customer pays, you are told through your dashboard [[and by SMS (not yet available)]]. You
see the order, the item subtotal and the delivery address needed to fulfil it. You do **not** receive
the customer's contact details and **may not contact the customer directly**; use AshantiHub
Support (Part A, section 4).

## 3. Fulfilling

Prepare orders promptly. Delivery is by an AshantiHub rider or an independent rider; hand goods only
to the rider assigned to the order. For store pickup, the customer collects at the **AshantiHub
office**, not at your shop.

## 4. Platform fee

4.1 AshantiHub charges **you**, the vendor, a platform fee on each order. **The delivery fee is
never charged to you.**

4.2 The fee is calculated on your **item subtotal after your own discounts, excluding delivery**.
Coupons funded by AshantiHub are not deducted from your fee base.

4.3 For products the fee is currently:

| Your item subtotal on the order (GHS) | Platform fee |
|---|---|
| 0.00 to 99.99 | 1% |
| 100.00 to 499.99 | GHS 5.00 flat |
| 500.00 to 49,999.99 | 1% |
| 50,000.00 and above | GHS 500.00 flat |

Services and events have their own fee tables [[to be set before launch]]; stays and bookings are
treated as services. In a cart with several vendors, each vendor is charged on their own portion.

4.4 There is a **lifetime cap** on the total fees you pay [[cap, if any]]. When a fee would take you
past the cap, you pay only the remainder.

4.5 The fee that applies to an order is fixed when the customer pays. Later changes to the fee
tables apply only to later orders. We announce fee changes before they take effect.

## 5. Your balances

Your wallet shows two balances:

- **Pending:** money from paid orders that has not yet been released to you. It cannot be
  withdrawn.
- **Available:** released money, after the platform fee. You can withdraw it (Part D).

## 6. When we release your money

6.1 We do not hold your money for long. Once the order has been **delivered** (or collected), the
customer has **confirmed receipt** (or told us on the call), and an AshantiHub Support officer has
**called the customer and confirmed the order matches what was bought**, we release the money at
once: your item subtotal, less the platform fee, moves from Pending to Available.

6.2 If the customer says the order does **not** match, a **dispute** is opened and nothing is
released while it is open. We aim to settle disputes within **72 hours of purchase**. The outcome
may be release to you, a refund to the customer, or a part of each.

6.3 If we cannot reach the customer, the order stays open and **nothing is released
automatically**. Support will keep following up.

6.4 Services, events and stays follow the same approach: the service is done, the event has taken
place or the stay has begun, the customer confirms, and we verify by call [[exact trigger for each
kind to be confirmed]].

## 7. Refunds and recovery

7.1 If a refund is approved **after** we have released money to you, we **debit your Available
balance** for the refund and return the matching share of the platform fee to you.

7.2 Your Available balance **may go negative** because of such a refund. A negative balance is
**recovered from your future sales**, and **you cannot withdraw** while it is negative.

7.3 If you run a **free-delivery promotion**, the cost AshantiHub bears for the waived delivery fee
is taken from your Available balance when the order is released, and returned to you if the whole
order is refunded.

## 8. Statements

Your transactions page shows each order, the gross amount, the platform fee shown separately, the
amount due and its status (Pending, Available, Withdrawn or Refunded), with your lifetime fee cap
used and remaining, and an export [[and per-order SMS (not yet available)]].

# Part D: Wallet Terms (customers and vendors)

## 1. What the wallet is

1.1 Your AshantiHub wallet is a **record** of money that belongs to you, held for you by the
Provider. For a customer it shows refunds and top-ups. For a vendor it shows Pending and Available
balances.

1.2 The wallet is **not a bank account**. AshantiHub is not a bank, and the wallet does not earn
**interest**. Money in your wallet is not insured by a deposit-protection scheme [[confirm
wording with legal and Bank of Ghana guidance]].

1.3 You can use the wallet only on AshantiHub and to withdraw to your own verified account.
Wallet balances cannot be transferred to another AshantiHub user.

1.4 [[Maximum wallet balance, single-transaction limit and daily limit, as set with Bank of Ghana
guidance.]]

## 2. Topping up

2.1 Customers can top up the wallet through the Provider's checkout by mobile money. You pay the
Provider's collection charge at cost, shown as "payment processing". A top-up is credited only when
the Provider confirms it.

2.2 Top-ups must come from an account in your own name, and be funds you are entitled to use.

## 3. Paying from the wallet

You can pay for an order from your wallet if the balance covers the whole order. There is no
processing charge.

## 4. Refunds into the wallet

Refunds go to the customer's wallet as set out in Part B, section 6.

## 5. Payout accounts

5.1 To withdraw, you must first add a **payout account**: a mobile money number or bank account.

5.2 We check that the **name on the account matches your verified name** (your profile name for a
customer; the verified owner or business name for a vendor). If it does not match, we refuse the
account. You can only withdraw to an account in your own name.

5.3 You confirm a new payout account with a **one-time code**.

5.4 A new or changed account cannot receive money for a **cooling period** (currently 24 hours).
Payout accounts are not edited: to change one, remove it and add a new one, which starts a new
cooling period. We notify you whenever a payout account is added or removed. If you did not do this,
contact Support immediately.

## 6. Withdrawing

6.1 You can withdraw your available wallet balance to a verified payout account that has finished
its cooling period. We ask for a **one-time code** each time.

6.2 There are **limits** on each withdrawal and per day [[limits]]. A vendor with a negative
Available balance cannot withdraw.

6.3 The Provider charges a **withdrawal charge**. **You bear it**; it is deducted from the amount
sent to you, and we show it before you confirm.

6.4 Withdrawals are sent through the Provider. A withdrawal is "in transit" until the Provider
confirms it. If it **fails**, the money returns to your wallet. If the Provider confirms it was
paid, it is not returned. We never send the same withdrawal twice.

6.5 We may delay, hold or refuse a withdrawal that looks suspicious, or while a dispute, hold or
investigation is open, and we will tell you why unless the law or an investigation prevents us.

## 7. Mistakes and unauthorised use

7.1 Check your wallet and notices regularly. Tell Support **immediately** if you see something you
did not authorise, or if you lose your phone or SIM.

7.2 If AshantiHub makes an error in the ledger, we will correct it by a new entry, and tell you if it
affects you. If money was credited to you by mistake, we may reverse it.

## 8. Closing your wallet

You may ask Support to close your account. We will pay out any available balance to a verified
payout account, subject to disputes, holds and legal requirements. Money in dispute stays held
until resolved.
